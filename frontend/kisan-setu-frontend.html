Direct Traceability Protocol

From farm gate to city buyer in four transparent steps

Replacing opaque middleman commission auctions with algorithmic fair pricing, direct buyer contracts, and shared multi-stop transport.

01
🧑‍🌾

Farmer Lists Produce

Enter crop, harvest date, location, and quantity. The ML engine forecasts 7-day modal prices and recommends an optimal farmgate asking price.

02
🤖

AI Price Discovery

Trained on 48,000+ AGMARKNET mandi records, the model incorporates seasonality, arrival elasticity, and quality grade multipliers with confidence bands.

03
🛒

Buyer Direct Order

Retailers and bulk institutions purchase directly from verified farmer listings at ~15% below retail rates, locking quantities and delivery slots.

04
🗺️

OSRM Corridor Logistics

Shared freight algorithms optimize multi-farm pickups and multi-drop routes with OpenStreetMap road routing, verified by live custody tracking.
